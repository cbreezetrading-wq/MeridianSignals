## Defects found during verification (both fixed, both have regression proof)

1. **Control 2 cancelled protective orders.** `cancelPendingOrders` cancelled every
   Meridian-tagged PENDING order, and OANDA reports STOP_LOSS/TAKE_PROFIT orders as
   PENDING. It cancelled the stop and target of a live trade, leaving it naked.
   Fixed: the control refuses protective order types and orders carrying a `tradeID`,
   and verifies its own side effects (protection snapshot before/after) before it
   reports success. Regression proof: `07-controls.json` (`untouchedProtective`,
   `protectionsPreserved: true`).
2. **Close events were written without an order key**, so reconciliation believed a
   closed trade was still open. Fixed in `closeManagedPositions`; historical records
   were repaired by appending correctly-keyed CLOSED records (never by editing).

Also measured, not assumed: OANDA does **not** copy an order's `clientExtensions` onto
the trade it opens. Managed identity therefore comes from the protective legs' client
extensions plus the record's known trade ids.
